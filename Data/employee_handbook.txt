Employee Handbook

Employees receive 12 sick leaves every year.

Employees receive 20 casual leaves every year.

Office timings are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees can work from home two days a week with manager approval.

The HR department is located on the third floor.

Medical insurance covers spouse and two dependent children.

Employees should submit leave requests at least two days in advance.

The company follows a hybrid work model.
