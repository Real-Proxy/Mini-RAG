Employee Handbook

Welcome to ABC Technologies!

We are excited to have you as part of our team. This handbook provides an overview of the company's policies, work culture, and employee benefits.

--------------------------------------------------
Office Hours
--------------------------------------------------

The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to log in before 9:15 AM. Repeated late arrivals may be discussed with the reporting manager.

--------------------------------------------------
Hybrid Work Policy
--------------------------------------------------

Employees are allowed to work from home up to two days per week with prior approval from their reporting manager.

Employees working remotely must remain available during working hours and attend all scheduled meetings.

--------------------------------------------------
Leave Policy
--------------------------------------------------

Employees receive the following leave benefits every calendar year:

- 20 Casual Leave days
- 10 Sick Leave days
- 12 Public Holidays

Leave requests should be submitted at least two working days in advance whenever possible.

Medical leave extending beyond three consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

Unused Casual Leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

--------------------------------------------------
Payroll
--------------------------------------------------

Salary is credited on the last working day of every month.

Employees can access salary slips through the employee self-service portal.

Any payroll-related concerns should be reported to the Finance department within five working days.

--------------------------------------------------
Performance Reviews
--------------------------------------------------

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year.

Employees are evaluated based on technical skills, teamwork, communication, ownership, and overall contribution to projects.

Outstanding performers may receive bonuses or salary revisions based on company policy.

--------------------------------------------------
Training
--------------------------------------------------

Every new employee must complete the mandatory onboarding program during the first week.

The company provides access to online learning platforms for technical and professional development.

Employees are encouraged to complete at least two certification courses every year.

--------------------------------------------------
IT Policies
--------------------------------------------------

Each employee is assigned a company laptop during onboarding.

Installing unauthorized software is prohibited.

Company devices should always be protected with strong passwords and automatic screen lock.

Employees must immediately report lost or stolen devices to the IT department.

--------------------------------------------------
Information Security
--------------------------------------------------

Company information should never be shared with unauthorized individuals.

Employees must not store confidential company documents on personal cloud storage services.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts.

Passwords should not be shared with anyone, including colleagues.

Employees should immediately report phishing emails or suspicious activity to the Information Security team.

--------------------------------------------------
Code of Conduct
--------------------------------------------------

All employees are expected to maintain a professional and respectful workplace.

Harassment, discrimination, and inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated.

Employees should communicate respectfully with colleagues, clients, and partners.

--------------------------------------------------
Travel Policy
--------------------------------------------------

Business travel must be approved by the reporting manager before bookings are made.

Travel expenses such as flights, hotels, and local transportation are reimbursed according to company policy.

Employees should submit reimbursement claims within 15 days after completing business travel.

--------------------------------------------------
Office Facilities
--------------------------------------------------

The HR department is located on the third floor.

Meeting rooms can be booked through the internal booking portal.

The cafeteria operates from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Free coffee, tea, and drinking water are available throughout the day.

The office provides free high-speed Wi-Fi for all employees.

--------------------------------------------------
Resignation Policy
--------------------------------------------------

Employees are required to provide a notice period of 60 days before leaving the company.

During the notice period, employees are expected to complete knowledge transfer and hand over assigned responsibilities.

Company-issued assets including laptops, ID cards, and access cards must be returned before the final working day.
